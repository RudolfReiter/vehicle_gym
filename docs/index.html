Experimental protocol

Six vehicles share one periodic, curvilinear track model and identical vehicle dynamics. Each MPC receives all five measured opponent states, solves its own finite-horizon problem, then applies only the first force and steering-rate input. All six vehicles advance simultaneously. Opponent predictions are assumptions inside each optimization, not their actual future controls.

Policy Control architecture
MPC 01–06 Independent nonlinear MPC, individual target speeds and lateral references

All six racing policies use fixed references and weights, with different target speeds and lateral offsets. There is no reinforcement learning in this experiment. Each current MPC plan responds to traffic and circuit curvature. Obstacle trajectories update at every control step; after a pass, the follower is assumed able to brake. This feasibility assumption does not assert that the follower intends to brake.

Numerical implementation and reproducibility

acados SQP, the original vehicle_gym kinematic Frenet model, soft state and obstacle constraints, and an approximate terminal condition. Track coordinates come from the packaged IMS and Sepang datasets, periodically smoothed over 6 m. Published failures remain in the evaluation. The 25-interval, 5-second horizon is a numerical recreation; it does not establish recursive safety.
